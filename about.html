About Palazzo Antico

Where preserved history meets heartfelt hospitality

The Garden Within the Walls

A House of Life and Rebirth

It began with a spark of **life**. **Vito** brought that energy from Italy, and **Renée** nurtured it with a spirit of **renewal** from France. Together, they looked at the crumbling medieval walls of Rhodes and saw not a ruin, but a beginning. They planted the first seeds of the future in this stone courtyard.

Then came the generation of **rebellion**. **Miriam** refused to accept the frantic pace of the modern world. Along with **Ennio**, a soul who had **wandered** the Aegean searching for truth, she found this castle. Here, they stopped. They turned their backs on the ordinary and chose a different path—creating a slow, authentic home where the days moved with the sun, not the clock. For twenty years, they guarded this sanctuary.

**Rocco and Marta** grew up sheltered by this legacy. They were the harvest of that patience. When they came of age, they didn't want to keep this magic to themselves. They founded **Aria di Rodi** to open the heavy wooden doors and let the world share in the warmth they had known all their lives.

And the story continues to move. Today, you will hear the footsteps of **Federico, Andrea, and Athena**—the fourth generation—running through the garden. They are the energy that keeps this place young.

Aria di Rodi is more than a hotel. It is a family chronicle of life, renewal, and the rebellious choice to build a home. We invite you to be part of it.

Philosophy

Authentic Hospitality

We believe that true hospitality is born from authenticity. Every element of your stay—from the original stone walls to the herbs growing in our courtyard—tells a genuine story.

We don't manufacture experiences; we curate connections. Connections to this place's extraordinary history, to our community of local artisans and producers, and to the timeless rhythms of Mediterranean life.

At Aria di Rodi, you're not just a guest—you're a temporary custodian of a living heritage, welcomed into a home that has sheltered travelers for over five centuries.
